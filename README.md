 INDIAN-MATERIVERSE-CONSTRUCTION
A sustainable construction material delivery platform developed using Design Thinking methodology. The project focuses on eco-friendly construction materials, supplier management, real-time delivery tracking, and efficient procurement solutions.
Indian Materiverse Construction

## Overview
Indian Materiverse Construction is a Design Thinking based project focused on developing a smart and sustainable construction material delivery platform.

The platform helps users purchase construction materials easily with better pricing, quality assurance, supplier management, and real-time delivery tracking.

---

## Problem Statement
Many people face difficulties while purchasing construction materials because of:
- High material costs
- Poor quality products
- Lack of transportation support
- Limited awareness of sustainable materials
- Difficulty comparing prices and specifications

---

## Proposed Solution
We designed a digital platform that:
- Connects suppliers and customers
- Provides eco-friendly construction materials
- Enables real-time inventory tracking
- Offers delivery tracking using GPS
- Supports bulk ordering
- Simplifies material procurement

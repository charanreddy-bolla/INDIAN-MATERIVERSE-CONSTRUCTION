About Project

Indian Materiverse Construction is a Design Thinking based project developed to simplify construction material procurement and delivery.


The project focuses on sustainable construction practices, supplier coordination, inventory management, smart logistics, and eco-friendly material solutions.


Technologies and concepts used:

  • Cloud Computing
  • IoT Concepts
  • GPS Tracking
  • Supply Chain Management
  • Design Thinking Methodology
